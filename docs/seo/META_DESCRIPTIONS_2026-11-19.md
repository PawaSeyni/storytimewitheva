**How:**
- **10 rewritten by hand** (marked ✍︎ below): the home, privacy and classroom pages in three languages and *The Tower That Touched the Sky*. These are written for search, so they were shortened to keep their point, not cut.
- **115 fitted automatically** from text the page also shows (activity blurbs, collection, unit and journey intros). `src/lib/metaDescription.ts` keeps the whole sentences that fit in 155 characters; when even the first sentence is too long (34 pages, marked …), it cuts at a word and adds an ellipsis. The text visitors see on these pages is unchanged.

**Owner review:** please read the ES and FR rows, above all the ✍︎ ones. Any wording you want changed, tell me the page and the new text; a hand-written description can replace any automatic one.
